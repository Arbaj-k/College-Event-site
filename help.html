Help

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I find events?

Open Events to browse listings. Use the search field or category filters to narrow the results.

How do I register?

Choose an event and select Register Now while registration is open. The registration form opens in a new tab.

Why can't I register?

Registration may be closed because the deadline has passed or the event has ended. Contact the organizer if you need clarification.

How can an event be listed?

Ask the college event administrators to publish it. You can contact collegeevent28@gmail.com.
