About Hapn

One place for everything happening on campus.

Hapn helps students find workshops, competitions, hackathons, cultural nights, sports meets and career sessions in one place, so nothing gets lost in a crowded group chat.

How it works

  1. Find an eventBrowse the list or search by name, venue or category.
  2. Tap Register NowThe event's Google Form opens in a new tab.
  3. Fill in the formThe organizers receive your response directly. There is no account to create here.

Organizing an event?

Ask the college event administrators to list it. Send them the event name, date, time, venue, a poster and your Google Form link. Contact: collegeevent28@gmail.com
